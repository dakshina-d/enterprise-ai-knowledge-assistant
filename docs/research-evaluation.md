# Recursive-research final-pipeline evaluation

`py -3.12 -m enterprise_ai.research.cli evaluate` executes the 12 committed benchmark questions offline. Each question uses its requested authenticated role, the compiled LangGraph route, local BM25 through the offline hybrid-compatible adapter, fake structured planning and generation, bounded recursive workers, restricted typed Python analysis where authorized, final evidence validation, grounded response generation, bounded citation repair, final citation validation, and bounded session-memory update. It makes no Pinecone or OpenAI call, requires no credential, and writes no evaluation artifact.

The safe per-question record includes the question ID and role; selected route; initial/child task and maximum-depth counts; retrieval, analysis and LLM calls; authorized/excluded evidence; structured conflicts; coverage and completion status; factual/analytical claims; final citations and citation-error categories; analytical-provenance validation; unsupported analytical claims; fallback use; and limitation/gap count. It does not print evidence bodies or inaccessible identifiers.

Final factual claims are checked against the exact authorized final evidence context and rendered citation map. The check requires a known model citation ID and matching evidence, chunk, document, source, line span and current ingestion build fingerprint. Unauthorized, unknown, stale, or mismatched citations fail integrity. The existing deterministic validator establishes structural attribution and claim-to-context support; it does not claim general semantic entailment.

Typed analytical results are not forced into document citations. Their provenance check requires the operation, formula, authorized/excluded row counts, algorithm version, applicable taxonomy version, and source/support identifiers produced by the restricted tool. Provider prose cannot alter calculated values. An analytical-provenance token is never accepted as a document citation.

Citation-validation pass rate is `validly cited final factual claims / final factual claims`. Safe limitation-only answers with no factual claim are excluded from that denominator. Evidence-context availability is the fraction of questions with at least one final authorized evidence item and is reported separately. Recall@1/3/5 and MRR are document-retrieval metrics, not citation correctness or complete answer quality.

The command exits non-zero for authorization violations, unauthorized/stale/unknown/invalid accepted citations, or unsupported analytical claims. Partial, insufficient, authorization-blocked and budget-exhausted answers do not fail merely for being honest. The deterministic CI provider is stable. A three-worker compiled fixture exercises completion orders `T02,T03,T01`, `T01,T02,T03`, and `T03,T01,T02`; only runtime IDs, timestamps and durations are normalization candidates, while plan, tasks, budgets, result/ledger order, provenance, analysis, conflicts, coverage, citations, final response and memory-safe turn remain substantive comparison fields.

Limitations: the benchmark uses offline BM25 rather than Pinecone; fake generation produces deliberately minimal grounded prose; structural citation validation is not universal semantic fact checking; public worker events remain deterministic post-fan-in projections; FastAPI/SSE delivery, MCP, human approval, reranking and Streamlit remain pending. Real LangSmith export was manually verified using a temporary user-provided credential that was removed after testing.
